# Customer Experience hero

`CustomerConversationHero` in `components/SolutionHeroScenes.tsx` renders a conversation-focused hero. Its `xc-*` styles live in `app/solution-hero-scenes.css`, sharing Satoshi, sapphire actions and the marketing motion controls. The previous CustomerExperienceHero component and its stylesheet are retired. Surrounding workflows, demo modal, navigation and footer remain unchanged.

Delivery, Product and Policy exception buttons select three fictional conversations. Each runs five stages: listen, context, guidance, draft and review. The exception prepares a human-specialist handoff. A large tilted conversation surface, separate review receipt and approved penguin illustration form the composition. No processed-task counter, live customer message or account connection is implied.

The sequence reaches review after 3.4 seconds and holds for 1.9 seconds before replay. Manual stage selection pauses; Replay restarts. Pause, reduced-motion manual stepping, off-screen and hidden-tab suspension remain available. The scene stacks on mobile; mascot placement stays clear of response text. No API requests or messages are sent.
